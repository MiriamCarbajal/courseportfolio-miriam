My Favorite Albums

These albums each have a memory or a time period of my life that I associate them with. I really enjoy listenign to albums vs putting together my own playlist because I like to think that the artist has a story or message they want to deliver with each album and that adds to the experience for me.

Karma

Album | 2018 | Summer Walker | R&B

This is the first album that I listened to from Summer Walker. The album is really good and it has songs that are a bit sad but I think that's what made them so relatable to me at the time. Some people like to listen to happy music when they're sad but I like to listen to music that can help me process the emotions that I am feeling. I don't listen to Summer Walker that much anymore. She had another great album after this one but this album is definitely the one that I listened to most and have the most memories with.

Visitor

Album | 2026 | Sienna Spiro | Pop

Although this album is recent, I have had it on repeat since it came out, and I can confidenely say that It's one that I will remember fondly. I first discovered Sienna Spiro through a Tik-tok trend that was going around about one of her songs. Her voice is very unique and I really enjoy this album, and I am excited to see what else she will release in the near future.

Trilogy 3

Album | 2012 | The Weeknd | R&B

This album I think is my top favorite of all time. I have even have gone as far as wanting to get a refrence from the album tattoed. I still go through periods in time where I listen to the album on repeat, and I love it as much as I did when I first heard it. I love anything The Weeknd releases, but this album is my favorite of them all.

Cowboy Like Me

Album | 2014 | Cody Johnson | Country

I liked country music before thi album, but this album is the first that I remember being obssessed with and having on repeat constantly. I am still a big fan of Cody Johnson, I almost went to see him in concert when he came for the Houston Rodeo, but I was unable to due to arragnements I already had for that day. The songs on this album are more upbeat, so this album I like to listen to when I am in the mood for a fun country song.

SOS

Album | 2022 | SZA | R&B & Hip Pop

I think this album is what got me through 2022. I had everyone in my life annoyed with me because I played this album on repeat just about all year. I enjoyed listeing to SZA before this and even now I realy like her new album. I really believe this album is one of her best.

$ad Boyz 4 Life 2

Album | 2021 | Junior H | Regional Mexican

This album is from a Mexican aritist and although the title is in english, the songs on the album are in spanish. This is another album that I loved so much that I almost went to get a tattoo in refrence to it. I have seen Junior H in concert twice and it was a great experience each time.
